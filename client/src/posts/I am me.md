One time, a girl told me that I had it all together.

And I thought yeahhh you right.

One time, I noticed noticing.

And nothing was the same. 

One time, I saw a photo of myself standing on a hill.

I did not recognize it.

One time, there was a bug in my room. 

I left it alone. 

One time, I hit someone, really fuckin hard. 

And part of me felt bad for the guy.

One time I kicked a rock, and the damn thing kicked me back. 

And another time, I held my own hand.

Oh\! One time, actually, quite a few times, I wore jeans and no underwear. 

One time on a plane, I threw up on this couple that was sitting beside me. They were coming back from their honeymoon and it wasn’t funny. 

One time I said I wish I wasn’t me. 

One time, I played God. 

I don’t do that anymore. 

One day, someone I do not know will ask me who I am.

And I will tell them I am me.
